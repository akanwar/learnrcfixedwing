Chapter 04

The Worked Trainer

One airplane, designed end-to-end with every number on the table: 1.5 kg, 1.47 m span, gentle and honest. Steal these numbers for your first build.

Step 1 — the mass budget

Target all-up mass1500 gThe number everything below uses

At every review, replace estimates with measured masses. If the total grows, re-run wing loading, stall speed, power, and CG.

Step 2 — size the wing

S = 1500 / 45 = 33.3 dm²  ·  b = √(6.5 × 0.333) = 1.47 m  ·  c = 0.333 / 1.47 = 226 mm

Target 45 g/dm² at AR 6.5. With CLmax ≈ 1.1: Vs ≈ 8.1 m/s (29 km/h), approach ≈ 10.5 m/s. Rectangular or mildly tapered planform for predictable building and stall.

Step 3 — size the tail and controls

Tail arm 650 mm, Vh = 0.55, Vv = 0.04:

Sh ≈ 640 cm² (470 × 135 mm)  ·  Sv ≈ 300 cm² (240 tall × 125 mm)

Elevator ≈30% of tail chord (40 mm, full span). Rudder 35–40% of fin chord. Ailerons 45 mm × ≈350 mm each, outer half-wing, 1.5–2:1 differential.

Step 4 — power and maiden setup

P = 150 W/kg × 1.5 kg ≈ 225 W  ·  I ≈ 225 / 11.1 ≈ 20.3 A  ·  endurance ≈ 0.8 × 2.2 / 12 × 60 ≈ 8.8 min
  • 3S 2200 mAh; documented motor/prop combo near 220–240 W; ESC ≥30 A with adequate BEC.
  • Maiden CG conservative (10–15% SM); low rates; directions checked from behind the plane.
  • An experienced pilot flies or assists; climb to height before trimming or stall tests.
Current limits are maximums, not targets. Verify volts, amps, watts and temperatures with a wattmeter — cowling on.
